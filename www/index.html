The Go Clock

The Go Clock is a virtual goban where the stones are used to tell you the time, saving you from the effort of setting up and continually updating your own Go board.

Swipe across the board to change the clock face, and across the background to change the background. Hold a finger on the board for a moment and it becomes a hand: drag it to push the stones about.

With a keyboard: the left and right arrows change the face, up and down the background, and M mutes the stones.
